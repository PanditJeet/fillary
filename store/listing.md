## Full description (max 4000 characters)
```
Fillary is a calm, tap-to-fill coloring book for kids and grown-ups. Pick a picture, choose a color, tap — and watch your artwork come alive. No mess, no stress, no going outside the lines.

🎨 TAP TO FILL
Just tap any area to fill it with color. Colors stay perfectly inside the lines, so every picture looks great — whether you're 4 or 94.

🐶 24 HAND-DRAWN PICTURES
Color adorable animals, fun kid characters and magical friends:
• Cute animals – a playful puppy, a kitten with yarn, a bunny, a baby panda, a fox, a lion cub, a hamster and a dolphin with a sea turtle
• Kids & adventures – an astronaut, a chef, a superhero, a gardener, a ballerina, a knight, a scuba diver, a scientist and more
• Magical friends – a baby unicorn, a wizard owlet, a moon fox and a starry baby dragon

🌈 BEAUTIFUL PALETTES
Choose from 6 hand-picked color palettes — or pick any color you like with the custom color picker. Your recently used colors are always one tap away. Unlock bonus palettes by watching a short optional video.

↩️ UNDO & REDO
Changed your mind? Undo and redo your fills any time, so coloring always stays relaxing.

🔍 ZOOM IN FOR DETAILS
Pinch to zoom and drag to move around the picture to color even the smallest areas.

💾 AUTOMATIC SAVING
Your progress is saved automatically. Come back anytime and continue right where you left off.

🖼️ SAVE & SHARE YOUR ART
Save your finished artwork to your gallery and share it with family and friends.

🎵 SOOTHING SOUNDS
Gentle wind-chime music and soft "water drop" fill sounds help you relax. Turn them on or off any time.

WHY FILLARY?
• Simple enough for young children, relaxing for adults
• No sign-up, no account needed
• Your coloring stays on your device
• Free to play, supported by family-friendly ads

Grab your favorite picture and start coloring — it's your quiet moment of calm.
```

## Data safety (suggested answers; check them against the latest Google form)
- **Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types?** Yes, through the Google Mobile Ads SDK.
- **Data types:**
  - *Device or other IDs*: collected and shared, used for **Advertising or marketing** and **Fraud prevention**. Note that the app does **not** use the Advertising ID; the SDK may use app-set IDs.
  - *App info and performance → Crash logs / Diagnostics*: collected by the ads SDK for **Analytics** (ad performance).
  - *App activity → App interactions*: collected and shared for **Advertising**.
  - *Location → Approximate location* (derived from IP address): collected for **Advertising** and **Fraud prevention**.
- **Is data encrypted in transit?** Yes.
- **Can users request that data be deleted?** Coloring data is stored only on the device and is deleted by clearing app data or uninstalling.
- **Data collected by the app itself (outside of ads):** none.
