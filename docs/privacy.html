Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Fillary coloring app for Android ("Fillary", "the app"), developed by EasyBitz Studios ("we", "us"), handles information. Fillary is designed to be used by children and adults alike, and we keep data collection to the minimum needed to run the app and show ads.

In short
  • No account, no sign-up, and we don't ask for your name, email, photos or contacts.
  • Your coloring progress and settings stay on your device.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. They are limited to family-friendly content, are not personalized, and do not use the Advertising ID.

1. Information stored on your device

Fillary saves the following only on your device, so you can continue where you left off:

  • your coloring progress (which areas you filled and with which colors) and small preview images of your artwork;
  • app settings such as sound and music preferences, the selected palette, recent colors and unlocked palettes.

This information is not sent to us. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data in Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

2. Saving and sharing artwork

When you tap Export, Fillary saves a picture of your artwork to your device's gallery (in "Pictures/Fillary", on Android 10 and newer) and opens the Android share menu. The image is only shared if you choose an app to share it with. Fillary does not upload your artwork anywhere.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Fillary is free and supported by ads provided by Google AdMob. To protect younger users, every ad request from Fillary is:

  • tagged as child-directed (under the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act, COPPA) and as coming from a user under the age of consent (for the EEA and UK);
  • limited to ads rated suitable for general audiences;
  • made without the Android Advertising ID, which the app does not access.

As a result, Google serves non-personalized ads only. To show ads, prevent fraud and measure ad performance, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may still process limited technical information such as the device's IP address (used for approximate location), device type, operating system version, app version and ad interaction data (for example, whether an ad was shown or tapped). This processing is carried out by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. You can learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Some features, such as unlocking a bonus color palette, are offered in exchange for watching an optional rewarded video ad.

4. Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you use Fillary in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app may show Google's consent message before ads are loaded. You can change your choice at any time using Privacy Settings at the bottom of the app's home screen (shown when this applies to you).

5. Children's privacy

Fillary does not knowingly collect personal information from children. There are no accounts, chat, social features or in-app purchases, and the app does not ask for any personal details. Ads are configured as described in section 3. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Permissions

  • Internet access: to load ads.

Fillary does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location or files. Saving artwork to the gallery on Android 10 and newer does not require a storage permission.

7. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it with third parties, other than the ad-related processing by Google described in section 3.

8. Data retention and deletion

Data stored on your device remains there until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. We do not keep copies on any server.

9. Security

Because your progress is stored locally and the app has no accounts, there is no personal data held by us that could be exposed. Communication with Google's ad servers uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
